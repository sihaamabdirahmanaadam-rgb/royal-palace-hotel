The Story of Royal Palace Hotel

Three Decades of Palatial Craftsmanship

Founded in 1995 by visionary hoteliers, the Royal Palace Hotel was conceived with a singular aspiration: to redefine luxury hospitality through architectural majesty and personalized intimacy. Built using classical European facades combined with modern comforts, our property has hosted royalty, dignitaries, and discerning guests from over 120 nations.

Uncompromising Standards

Every guest suite is an artisan showcase featuring hand-woven carpets, bespoke chandeliers, Italian marble baths, and acoustic privacy barriers. Our team undergoes rigorous hospitality training under French butler academies, ensuring every wish is met with grace and anticipation.

Sustainable Luxury

We proudly balance grandeur with ecological conscience. 100% of our energy is offset by certified renewable sources, zero single-use plastics are deployed on the property, and our organic kitchen sources fresh produce from regional sustainable farms.
